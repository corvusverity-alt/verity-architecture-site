Canadians Guide to Wealth Building Without Risk

by Richard Canfield and Jayson Lowe

★★★★★

I wouldn’t spend my hard-earned money on this book. Canfield and Lowe introduce infinite banking, which uses the cash value of a whole life insurance policy to help finance purchases. I came away with a general idea of what they were selling and too little detail to judge whether it was worth doing.

The book felt like an advertisement for the authors’ services. A guide with “Without Risk” in the title should explain what the strategy costs, how the borrowing works and where it can fall short. I wanted enough detail to weigh those trade-offs for myself. I’d skip this one.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 21, 2026
