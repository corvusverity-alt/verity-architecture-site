Wealthing Like Rabbits

by Robert R. Brown

★★★★★

If someone tells me they hate reading about money, this is the book I give them. I met Robert Brown at a signing in Peterborough, Ontario, when the book first came out. I still have that signed copy, and over the years I’ve bought more copies for friends and family who wanted to get a handle on their finances.

Brown makes compound growth easy to picture. You can see what happens when you put a little money aside regularly and give it decades to grow. His sense of humour keeps the explanations of debt, RRSPs and TFSAs from feeling like homework. Some of the jokes will land better than others, and experienced investors will know much of the material. For someone who’s been putting off their first money book, I’d give it more than five stars if I could.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
