Cuckooland

by Tom Burgis

★★★★★

Tom Burgis follows international dealmaker Mohamed Amersi through a world where the story people tell about a fortune can matter almost as much as the fortune itself. Political donations, high-profile philanthropy and disputes over reputation become part of the same investigation. The unsettling question is how much harder it can become to scrutinize someone once powerful institutions have welcomed them.

The book asks readers to keep track of cross-border deals, overlapping relationships and competing accounts. That density can slow the story, but it also shows why the work of establishing what happened takes so long. Readers drawn to the links between money and political access will find more to think about here than in a simple catalogue of scandals.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 21, 2026
