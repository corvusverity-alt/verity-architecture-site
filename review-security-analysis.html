Security Analysis

by Benjamin Graham and David Dodd · Sixth edition

★★★★★

I’ve owned this book for about a decade and still haven’t finished it. It’s also the best hard-copy reference I own for digging through a company’s financial statements. When I need to slow down and think about what reported earnings tell me, or what stands behind a bond, this is the kind of book I want within reach.

The sixth edition includes essays that connect Graham and Dodd’s methods to later markets. The underlying text still demands patience, and there are parts I’ve had to work through slowly. My five stars are for its depth as a reference. If you’re serious about valuing individual companies, it earns its space on the shelf even when you read it a chapter at a time.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
