About the Book

When the Build Is Done

Most financial books stop at accumulation. They teach you to build — and then leave you standing in front of something you don't know how to put down. This book begins where the building ends.

The Architect's Business Escape Strategies turns from the question "how do I grow this?" to the harder one: "how do I leave it well?" It is a book about thresholds — knowing when enough is enough, converting assets into income and time, simplifying what you've made, and handing it on without watching it unravel. No new accounts to open. No new positions to chase. Just the architecture of a clean exit.
