The Tibetan Book of the Dead

Traditional Tibetan Buddhist text

★★★★★

I had an extremely hard time understanding this book. It helps to know that it comes from a Tibetan Buddhist tradition in which passages are read aloud to someone who is dying or has recently died. That explains why the text speaks so directly to the person going through the transition, but I still struggled to follow it.

The ritual language and imagery were hard for me to make sense of without more background. If you're curious about the text, I'd start with an edition that explains those passages as you go. For me, it was a 2/5.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 21, 2026
