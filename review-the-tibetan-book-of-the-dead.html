The Tibetan Book of the Dead

Traditional Tibetan Buddhist text

This text makes more sense once you know it was meant to be heard. In Tibetan Buddhist practice, passages are read aloud to a dying or recently deceased person as guidance through the transition between death and rebirth. The direct addresses and recurring instructions have a purpose that can be easy to miss if you approach the book as a general introduction to Buddhism.

The imagery is dense, and the ritual language can leave a new reader looking for context. An edition with an introduction or commentary gives the unfamiliar terms somewhere to land. It’s a book to read slowly if you’re curious about how a religious tradition turns questions about death and fear into words that can be spoken at someone’s bedside.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 21, 2026
