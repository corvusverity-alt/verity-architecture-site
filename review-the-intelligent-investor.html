The Intelligent Investor

by Benjamin Graham · Revised edition

★★★★★

This was the first finance book I read, during the 2008 market turmoil. I still think about Mr. Market. Graham imagines a business partner who quotes you a price every day, and you decide whether to do anything with it. That was an especially useful idea to encounter when prices were swinging hard. It still holds up whenever a quote starts to feel like an instruction.

I give the book three stars and the central lessons five. My copy feels dated, and some of Graham’s examples take work to translate to today’s markets. Jason Zweig’s commentary in the revised edition helps with that. I’d come to it after a beginner’s guide and spend extra time with Mr. Market and the margin of safety.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
