The Art of Spending Money

by Morgan Housel

★★★★★

After a lifetime of saving for retirement, spending some of it can feel like breaking the habit that got you there. That’s where Housel’s follow-up is at its best. He asks what you’d spend money on if you could set aside what the neighbours might think.

Readers of The Psychology of Money will recognise some of the ideas about status and knowing when you have enough. Here, they’re put to work on a different decision. I’d especially recommend it to people in the financial independence and early retirement community who know how to build savings and are figuring out how to enjoy them.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
