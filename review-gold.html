Gold

by Matthew Hart

★★★★★

The detail that stayed with me was what an ounce of gold might have bought in ancient Rome. That comparison made me think about purchasing power across centuries. Hart looks at gold as something people have tested, traded and placed their trust in long before it became a line on a portfolio statement.

He also follows gold into modern mines and through the boom after the 2008 financial crisis. The price history belongs to the period Hart covered, but the reporting helped me see why I wanted precious metals represented in my own portfolios, whether through bullion, miners or a trust. I’d recommend it to anyone curious about why gold still commands the attention it does.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
