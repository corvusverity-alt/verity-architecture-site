Rich Family Smart Family

by Robert T. Kiyosaki

★★★★★

In many households, talking about money used to be considered rude. I’ve always thought that silence comes at a cost. Kids can grow up knowing they’re expected to earn a living without ever hearing how a family decides what it can afford or how a business earns its money.

Kiyosaki gives parents a way into those conversations by starting with familiar things like income, expenses and cash flow. The concepts will feel familiar to readers of Rich Dad Poor Dad. I’d recommend it to young families for the conversations it can start around the kitchen table, including how a business works and how children might one day build one of their own.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
