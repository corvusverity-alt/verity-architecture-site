Market Masters

by Robin R. Speziale

★★★★★

Market Masters is probably one of my wife’s favourite finance books on our shelf. What stayed with me was how differently the Canadian investors in these interviews chose stocks and managed risk. Reading their approaches side by side gave me ideas to try over the years as I figured out what suited me.

Speziale leaves much of the comparison to the reader. I’d have liked more synthesis between interviews, especially when two investors approach the same problem differently. If you already know the basics and want to understand how Canadian stock pickers actually make decisions, there’s plenty here to think about.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
