The Psychology of Money

by Morgan Housel

★★★★★

I read this while travelling in Vietnam in 2026, and it made me think about mistakes I’d made trading. When I wanted to hold or sell a position, how much of that decision came from the investment itself, and how much came from my reaction to the price?

Housel uses short stories to make ideas about luck, risk and compounding easy to remember. His point about unseen wealth stayed with me. The money someone has kept and invested is much harder to see than what they’ve bought. Readers looking for a budget or an investing plan will need another resource. I’d hand this to anyone who wants to understand the habits they bring to either one.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
