Ford Nation

by Rob Ford and Doug Ford

The Ford brothers built a political following in part by making municipal government sound like something you could argue about at the kitchen table. The language of saving taxpayers’ money and stopping the “gravy train” was easy to repeat, and the book shows how central that message was to their own understanding of Rob Ford’s appeal.

This is the Ford family’s account, with Doug telling much of the story. The family loyalty helps explain why the authors give some episodes the emphasis they do, especially when public controversy collides with private life. It’s worth reading for anyone trying to understand why the Fords connected with some Toronto voters and how the family understood the loyalty of those supporters.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 21, 2026
