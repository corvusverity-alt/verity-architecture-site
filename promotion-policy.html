Editorial & Sponsored Content Policy

Independent reviews

Books for our Editorial reviews are chosen by us and actually read by the reviewer. Verdicts may be favourable, mixed or critical. No one can pay for a review, a rating or a verdict. If we received a free copy, we say so in the review.

Sponsored features

Paid features are advertising. Each one is marked Sponsored Book Feature — Paid Promotion at the top of the page and in any social post. They are written from the materials the author provides and only make claims we can support. We don't invent quotes, reading experiences, awards or endorsements.

Feature swaps

A feature swap is an exchange of promotional features between authors, disclosed on both sides. We never exchange, buy or sell customer reviews or ratings on Amazon, Goodreads or any other retailer.

Our own books

Verity Architecture publishes books by Corvus Verity. Any mention of those titles is house promotion, never independent coverage.

No guarantees

We don't promise traffic, search rankings, reviews or sales from any package.

Revisions & refunds

Each paid package includes one factual or wording revision. If we decline a book before work begins, you receive a full refund. After drafting has started, contact us and we'll work with you on a fair resolution. We may decline books that are plagiarised, misleading, hateful or otherwise unsuitable.

Privacy

We collect only what we need to produce your feature: your name, email, book details, bio and cover. Covers are stored in a private location, used only for your feature, and deleted on request or within 12 months after publication. We never sell your information. Payments are handled by our checkout provider; we never see your card details.

Corrections & contact

Spot an error? Email Support@VerityArchitect.com and we'll correct it promptly.
