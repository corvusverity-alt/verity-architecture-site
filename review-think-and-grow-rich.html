Think and Grow Rich

by Napoleon Hill

★★★★★

Hill’s most practical ideas are also his simplest. Decide what you want, put it into words and find people who will help you keep working at it. His idea of a “mastermind” group gives persistence a social side. A small circle can challenge your thinking when your plans are still taking shape.

The book leans on success stories and makes sweeping claims about what thought can accomplish. It gives little attention to everyday money decisions. Its best audience is someone who has a goal in mind and could use a push to turn it into a plan.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
