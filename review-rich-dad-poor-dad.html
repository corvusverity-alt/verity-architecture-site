Rich Dad Poor Dad

by Robert T. Kiyosaki

★★★★★

I bought this at LAX waiting for a flight in February 2026. I wish someone had handed it to me at 18. Kiyosaki put words to doubts I already had about how much school teaches us about building a business or owning assets. His cash-flow lens made me rethink how I looked at property and how I was setting up my investment accounts. It helped shape some of the accounts I went on to write about in Financially Trapped and Portfolio Architecture.

The book gives readers a question worth asking about any purchase. What will it put into my pocket, and what will it cost me to carry? I gave it five stars for the way that question changed my thinking. Readers looking for a worked example of financing a property or evaluating the downside of borrowing will need to go further.

Reviewed by Corvus Verity · November 20, 2026
